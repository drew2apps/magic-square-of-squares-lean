# References

A map of the literature around the 3×3 magic square of squares and the methods used in this repository. Compiled on
25 November 2026 (UTC) by an AI review for Drew (the document "Magic square of squares: literature map and new
directions"), and copied here with its links and statuses; the last section was added by the session that wrote this
file. **Read by AI models only, not by a mathematician**: a status says whether a page opened and matched the
citation, not that its content was checked. A star `*` marks a work that no Markdown file of this repository named at
commit `9d0e7e3`. Where a sentence elsewhere in the repository says what a paper proves, that sentence, not this list,
says how it was read.

Status words: *Verified*: the page opened and matched the citation. *Partial*: the citation is known from another
page, but its own page could not be opened. *Not found*: no page turned up.
