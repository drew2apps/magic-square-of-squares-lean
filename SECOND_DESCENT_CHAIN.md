## The chain

**1. From a square to the five forms.** A magic square of squares (`IsMSS`, nine integer squares, the magic sum `T`) with ratio `m : n = (b + d) : (h + f)`, `h + f ≠ 0`, `b ≠ d`, gives `t = (h + f)/(b − d)` at which `F0, F8, F4, L, U` of `G1_FULL_SYSTEM.md` are rational squares (`IsMSS.fullSquares`, `G1Bridge.lean`). Writing `t = u/v` in lowest terms, these are the conditions `N(α0), N(α8), 2S·N(γ4), 2N(γL), 2N(γU)` square (`norm_*` in `GaussDescent.lean`). **[Lean]** The hypotheses `h + f ≠ 0`, `b ≠ d` are those of every census statement in Lean (stated exactly in `LAST_PAIR.md` §15; `IsMSS` assumes no distinctness). That the census list of nine is complete for `m ≤ 1000` is `IsMSS.ratio_ne_of_le_1000` and the census itself; this chain only concerns the seven named ratios.

**2. The global classes.** For each of the five Gaussian linear forms `ℓ_j`, its value at `(u, v)` is `δ_j·β_j²` with `δ_j` in an explicit finite set `D_j` (the Lemma of `GAUSS_DESCENT.md` §1: primes of `2mn` for `α0, α8`; only `1 + i` and units for `γ4` when `S` is a square or twice a square; `1 + i` times primes of `m² − n²` for `γL, γU`). The resultant half (a common prime of `ℓ` and `ℓ̄` divides `2mn`, `2`, `2(m² − n²)`) is **[Lean]** (`support_*`, `GaussDescent.lean`). The other half (an odd exponent at a split prime forces the conjugate, unique factorisation in `ℤ[i]`, units `±1, ±i`) is **[by hand]**, five lines in `GAUSS_DESCENT.md` §1 (a Lean version was written but not compiled; it is not part of this repository yet). `indep_class.py` derives the sets from the stated rule and checks that the resultants are the stated ones.

**3. The local step: only the two degenerate tuples survive.** For every prime `p` some primitive `(u, v) ∈ ℤ_p²` must realise the five classes at every place above `p`, and for each of the nine ratios exactly two of the 131,072 or 524,288 tuples survive the primes up to 200 and the bad primes: the tuples of the two degenerate solutions (`t = ±1` when `S` is a square, `t = 0, ∞` when `S` is twice a square), which are distinct. Ingredients: Hensel's lemma (a unit `≡ 1 mod π⁵` of `ℤ_2[i]` is a square; a unit of `ℤ_p` that is a residue is a square) **[by hand, standard]**; the patterns of all primitive pairs, including the scaling by `ℤ_p*` **[exact script, three implementations]**:
