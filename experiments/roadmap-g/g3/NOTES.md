## What it claims

For the two degenerate types only (`m² + n²` a square, or twice a square) and `11000 < m ≤ 13000`: the 611 coprime
ratios that pass the congruences, each with its first closing step and that step's label in `CENSUS_TABLE_G3.tsv`
(the format and labels of `../../census-wide/table/CENSUS_TABLE_1000_11000.tsv`). **Open:** (11165,7548), (12201,7360),
(12204,9853), (12703,2767) (the last is the partner of (7735,4968)). **Conditional on a rank being 1**, through partners
already conditional in that table: (11441,1519), (11969,4529), (12007,1799). (12416,5313) is closed by the rank-1 route,
conditional on (a), (b) as in `CENSUS9000.md`; (12487,4241) through its partner (8364,4123), closed by the route there.
Every rank bound is PARI's; every Demjanenko–Manin closure rests on PARI's generators (saturation at primes `≤ 1000`).
Ratios of other types with `m > 11000` are not covered.
